What is the chown command used for?

2.11 to 2.13


HTTP(Hypertext Transfer Protocol): Its used to transfer the data from one place to the other which could be other country.

- The data transfered using HTTP is not secure.

HTTPs(Hypertext transfer protocol secure): It's also used to transfer the data from one place to other place.

- The data which is transfered from HTTPs is secure.
